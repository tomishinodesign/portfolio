“I’m an industrial designer learning to code. I'm not sure where my design will go, but I want to fill the gap between what works and what matters.”

Design has never been more efficient or easier to produce. I want to see whether it’s possible to design for connection while maintaining the functional design of today.

I'm working towards building a connection between both the physical design of products and digital interfaces. I'm learning how to code so I can develop prototypes and allow others to quickly experience what something could look like in their own hands.
