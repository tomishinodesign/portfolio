How can we design an ironing system for less experienced users?

For decades, steam irons have kept a very similar form as the practices of ironing have been maintained. However, for infrequent users who find ironing a chore it can be a frustrating task.

User Journey Mapping

Jamie (21) is a university student living in Melbourne. Busy with a full course load, part-time job, and active social life, Jamie prioritises convenience and efficiency. Ironing is not part of Jamie's regular routine and is reserved for special occasions like presentations, interviews, or formal events. He wants to make a good impression with a quick, but straightforward ironing solution.
